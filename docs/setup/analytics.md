# Server Analytics

ArkenBot tracks activity across your server and displays it in an easy-to-read dashboard with charts and 24-hour stat cards.

## Viewing Analytics

Go to **Dashboard → Analytics** to see your server's data. No setup is required — analytics are collected automatically once the bot is in your server.

> Data is collected daily. Charts will begin populating after the first full day the bot is active.

## Stat Cards

Three real-time cards show the last 24 hours:

| Card | What it tracks |
|---|---|
| **New Members (24h)** | Members who joined in the past 24 hours |
| **Left Members (24h)** | Members who left in the past 24 hours |
| **Mod Actions (24h)** | Warnings, kicks, bans, and mutes issued |

## Charts (30-Day History)

### Member Growth
Line chart showing total member count over the past 30 days. Useful for spotting trends after events, giveaways, or partnership announcements.

### Activity
Bar chart showing daily message and command usage side by side. Helps identify your server's most and least active days.

### Member Flow
Bar chart showing daily joins vs. leaves. Use this to assess retention after recruitment drives or to spot unusual drop-off periods.

## Data Retention

Analytics history covers the past 30 days and is displayed per day. The dashboard refreshes automatically every 5 minutes.
